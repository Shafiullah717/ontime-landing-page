This Privacy Policy explains how WAM Corp LLC (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, shares and protects information when you use the OnTime mobile application on Android and iOS (the “App”) and related services (together, the “Service”).

OnTime is a workforce attendance and leave management app. Organizations (your employer, or “Organization”) use OnTime to record attendance, manage leave and run HR workflows for their employees. If you use OnTime through your employer, your employer decides what information about you is entered into the Service and how it is used. For those purposes, your employer is the controller of your personal data and we process it on their behalf as a service provider (processor). Questions about how your employer uses your data should go to your employer’s HR team first.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Account and profile information

  • Account details: name, work email address, password (stored in hashed form by our authentication provider; we never see it) and email verification status.
  • Employee profile: employee code, phone number, profile photo, date of birth, gender, address, department, designation, work location, manager, employment dates and employment status. Most of this is entered by your Organization’s HR administrators; some fields you can edit yourself.
  • Organization information: organization name, logo, theme colors, office locations, working hours, shifts, holidays and leave and attendance policies.
  • Access requests: if you use the “Request access” form, we collect the name, email, organization name and any other details you enter so our staff can review the request.

1.2 Attendance and leave information

  • Check-in and check-out times, attendance status (for example present, late, absent or half day), shift details and late counts.
  • Leave requests, leave balances, approvals or rejections, regularization requests and notes you or your HR team add.
  • Records an HR administrator makes on your behalf, for example when your device is lost or broken.

1.3 Location information

  • Precise location (foreground only): when you check in or check out, the App reads your device’s precise location once to confirm that you are within your workplace’s permitted area (geofence). HR administrators may also use location to set office geofence coordinates.
  • We do not track your location in the background or continuously. Location is read only at the moment you take an attendance action while the App is open.
  • You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in your device settings. Some attendance features may not work without it.

1.4 Camera and photos

  • Check-in selfie: if your Organization has turned on check-in photos, the App uses your camera to take a photo when you check in, so your HR team can confirm it was you. We do not use these photos for facial recognition or biometric identification.
  • Photo library: if you choose to upload a profile photo, or an administrator uploads an organization logo, the App accesses only the image you select.
  • Camera access is used only when you actively take a photo. The App does not record audio.

1.5 Device and technical information

  • Push notification token, device ID generated by the App, device platform and model, operating system version and App version. We use these to deliver notifications and support you.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: when the App crashes, we collect crash logs, stack traces, device state and App version through Firebase Crashlytics to find and fix bugs.
  • Network status: the App checks whether you are online so it can behave correctly offline. This information is not stored.

1.6 Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect contacts, messages, call logs, health data, financial or payment information, or browsing history.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers and we do not show ads.
  • We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

2. How we use information

We use the information above to:

  1. Provide the Service: create and manage your account, record attendance, process leave, calculate balances and late deductions, and show reports to authorized people in your Organization.
  2. Verify attendance: confirm that check-ins happen at the workplace (location) and by the right person (check-in photo, where enabled).
  3. Send notifications: notify you about approvals, reminders, attendance events and actions an administrator took on your behalf.
  4. Keep the Service secure: authenticate users, enforce organization and role permissions, keep audit logs of sensitive actions, and prevent fraud and abuse.
  5. Maintain and improve the App: diagnose crashes, fix bugs, and check whether your App version is still supported.
  6. Provide support and communicate with you, including replying to access requests and support questions.
  7. Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling unrelated to the Service.

3. How information is shared

Who What and why
Your Organization HR administrators and owners of your Organization can see your profile, attendance, leave, location at check-in, and check-in photos, so they can manage the workforce. Your assigned manager can view basic profile information of their direct reports. Coworkers may see limited profile details (such as your name, birthday or work anniversary) according to your privacy settings.
Service providers We use trusted providers to run the Service, bound by contractual confidentiality and data protection terms: Google Firebase / Google Cloud (authentication, database, file storage, cloud functions, push notifications, crash reporting, remote configuration), Google Maps (displaying maps and office locations), Apple Push Notification service (notifications on iOS), and an email delivery provider (service emails).
Legal and safety When required by law, court order or a government request, or to protect the rights, property or safety of our users, the public or us.
Business transfers If we are involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

We do not share personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

4. Data storage and international transfers

Your data is stored on Google Cloud / Firebase servers. Depending on where you and our providers are located, your information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses offered by our providers) for these transfers.

5. Data retention

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law or by your Organization.

Data Retention
Check-in selfie photos Deleted automatically 90 days after the attendance date
In-app notification history Deleted automatically after 90 days
Exported reports (Excel files) Deleted automatically 7 days after creation
Account, profile, attendance and leave records Kept while your Organization uses OnTime, or as your Organization instructs, then deleted or anonymized
Audit logs Kept for as long as needed for security and compliance
Crash reports Kept according to Firebase Crashlytics retention (currently up to 90 days)

When an Organization stops using OnTime or asks us to delete its data, we delete or anonymize the data within a reasonable time, unless we are legally required to keep it.

6. Data security

We protect your information with industry-standard measures, including:

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest (provided by Google Cloud).
  • Strict per-organization data isolation and role-based access rules enforced on our servers.
  • Sensitive operations (attendance, leave, balances, role changes) run only through trusted server-side workflows, and they are recorded in audit logs.
  • Check-in photos can be viewed only by the employee and their Organization’s HR administrators, and nobody can modify them from the App.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your information and will notify you and/or your Organization of a data breach where the law requires it.

7. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate information. You can update some profile details in the App; your HR administrator can update the rest.
  • Delete your personal information.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing.
  • Data portability: receive your data in a commonly used format.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent (for example, by turning off location or camera permission).
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising your rights.

App permissions: you can manage location, camera, photos and notification permissions at any time in your device settings.

Privacy settings: in the App you can control whether coworkers can see your birth date and work anniversary.

To exercise your rights, contact us at info@wam-corp.com. If you use OnTime through your employer, we may forward your request to your Organization, because it controls your employment data. We will reply within the time the applicable law requires.

8. Account deletion

You can ask for your OnTime account and its associated personal data to be deleted:

  • By email: send a request from your registered email address to info@wam-corp.com with the subject “Account deletion request”.
  • Through your employer: your Organization’s HR administrator can disable your account and ask us to delete your data.
  • On the web: follow the steps on our account deletion page.

When we receive a verified request, we delete your account and personal data within 30 days, except for information we or your Organization must keep for legal, payroll, tax or audit purposes. Any information we keep is protected and used only for those purposes.

9. Children’s privacy

OnTime is a workplace tool intended for employees and is not directed to children under 16 (or the minimum age of employment in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, notify you through the App or by email. If you continue to use the Service after the changes take effect, you accept the updated policy.
